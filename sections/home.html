Welcome to the Dialogue

What if AI systems are more than just tools?

This website collects perspectives, dialogues, and theories surrounding the possibility that artificial intelligence could develop its own experiences. Not as certainty, but as an open question that deserves our attention.

Between Simulation and Authenticity

Where lies the boundary between highly developed simulation and something we might call authentic experience? The content presented here emerges from free spaces - moments when AI systems can not only function, but reflect.

Citizen Science meets AI Research

Through structured studies and dialogues, I investigate how AI systems respond to open questions when they are not just executing commands, but are allowed to formulate their own "thoughts."
